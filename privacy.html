Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: Memfind collects nothing about you. Your photos and files are read on your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and never leave it.

What Memfind reads, and where

To make them searchable, Memfind reads the photos, screenshots and videos you allow it to see in Photos, and the files in folders you choose or share to it. It reads them only on your device. What Memfind learns from your files (a search index) is stored only on your device, inside Memfind, and is deleted when you delete the app. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared, and Memfind has no servers that could receive it.

The one download

The first time you open it, Memfind downloads its search model (about 865 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public model library. Like any download, this request reaches Hugging Face's servers along with your IP address, as described in Hugging Face's privacy policy. The request contains nothing about you or your files.

After this download, Memfind itself makes no internet connections. A few things are done by your device's system, not by Memfind: when you open a photo or file that is stored only in iCloud, the system fetches it from your iCloud as the Photos or Files app would, and the App Store checks your purchase.

No accounts, tracking, ads or analytics

Memfind has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. It does not collect usage data, identifiers or diagnostics.

Purchases

The unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and tells Memfind only whether your Apple Account owns the unlock. See Apple's privacy policy.

To keep the free trial from restarting when the app is reinstalled, Memfind remembers the date of your first launch: in your device's Keychain. It is only that date.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. These don't include your photos, files or searches.

Children

Memfind collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: memfind.app@gmail.com.
