Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: Memfind collects nothing about you. Your photos and files are read on your phone (iPhone or Android) and never leave it.

What Memfind reads, and where

To make them searchable, Memfind reads the photos, screenshots and videos you allow it to see (in Photos on iPhone, in your gallery on Android), voice recordings on Android, and the files in folders you choose or share to it. It reads them only on your phone. On Android, the words in screenshots are read by Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone; Memfind switches off the usage reports ML Kit would otherwise send to Google. What Memfind learns from your files (a search index) is stored only on your phone, inside Memfind, and is deleted when you delete the app. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared, and Memfind has no servers that could receive it.

The one download

The first time you open it, Memfind downloads its search model (about 865 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public model library. Like any download, this request reaches Hugging Face's servers along with your IP address, as described in Hugging Face's privacy policy. The request contains nothing about you or your files.

After this download, Memfind itself makes no internet connections. A few things are done by your phone's system, not by Memfind: on iPhone, when you open a photo that is stored only in iCloud Photos, iOS fetches it from your iCloud as the Photos app would; on both phones, the App Store or Google Play checks your purchase.

No accounts, tracking, ads or analytics

Memfind has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. It does not collect usage data, identifiers or diagnostics.

Purchases

On iPhone, the unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store; on Android, by Google through Google Play. The store handles payment and tells Memfind only whether your account owns the unlock. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

To keep the free trial from restarting when the app is reinstalled, Memfind remembers the date of your first launch: in the iPhone's Keychain, or on Android in Google's Block Store, which keeps it with your Google account. It is only that date.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. On Android, Google Play may share crash and performance statistics if you allow it in your phone's settings. These don't include your photos, files or searches.

Children

Memfind collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: memfind.app@gmail.com.
