Common questions

  • Why does Memfind download 865 MB first?

    That's its search model, which runs entirely on your device. It's downloaded once. Wi-Fi is best, and the download picks up where it stopped if it's interrupted.

  • How long does reading my library take?

    It depends on how many photos and files you have. Memfind reads while it’s open. On iPhone and iPad it keeps going if you tap “Keep going when I leave the app”, continues overnight while charging, and pauses by itself when the device is hot or the battery is low.

  • How do I add PDFs, documents or voice memos?

    On iPhone and iPad, open the ••• menu and choose Folders to pick a folder in the Files app. On Mac, choose folders in Memfind’s Settings. For a file in another app, tap Share and choose Memfind.

  • Memfind only sees some of my photos.

    You gave it access to selected photos. To change that, tap Change in Memfind, or go to Settings › Apps › Memfind › Photos (on Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos).

  • I bought the unlock but Memfind still asks.

    On iPhone and iPad, open the ••• menu › About Memfind › Restore Purchase; on Mac, choose Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with: one purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  • Refunds

    Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
