Common questions

Why does Memfind download 865 MB first?

That's its search model, which runs entirely on your iPhone. It's downloaded once. Wi-Fi is best, and the download picks up where it stopped if it's interrupted.

How long does reading my library take?

It depends on how many photos and files you have. Memfind reads while it's open, keeps going if you tap “Keep going when I leave the app”, and continues overnight while your iPhone charges. It pauses by itself when your iPhone is hot or the battery is low.

How do I add PDFs, documents or voice memos?

Open the ••• menu and choose Folders to pick a folder in the Files app. For a voice memo or a file in another app, tap Share and choose Memfind.

Memfind only sees some of my photos.

You gave it access to selected photos. To change that, go to Settings › Apps › Memfind › Photos, or tap Change in Memfind.

I bought the unlock but Memfind still asks.

Open the ••• menu › About Memfind › Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
